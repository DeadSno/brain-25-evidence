Privacy and cookies

Updated: 10.10.2026

The site collects a minimum of data. Web analytics (Yandex.Metrica) is enabled only after clicking “Accept” in the consent banner — before that, no request is sent to mc.yandex.ru.
Legal basis. Personal data on this site is processed in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27.07.2006 “On Personal Data” and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679). Below are the categories of data, the operators (third parties), retention periods and how to opt out.
Pages without personal data collection. The pages Comparison and Links work only with open scientific data on supplements (PubMed, CrossRef, OpenAlex, Wikimedia) and do not collect visitors' personal data.

📋 What data is collected

Automatically (when you visit the site)

  • IP address, cookies, on-site behaviour data;
  • via Yandex.Metrica (counter 112465667) — after clicking “Accept” in the consent banner.

The detailed composition of Metrica's technical data is in the section below.

Via the feedback form

  • name, email, message text;
  • via Yandex Forms (forms.yandex.ru) — the submission happens inside Yandex's form; no personal data is sent to our domain.

Email is provided voluntarily and used only to reply to your message. It is never published and never used for mailing lists.

🏛️ Who processes the data (third parties)

  • Yandex.Metrica — web analytics, counter 112465667. Policy: yandex.ru/legal/metrica_termsofuse
  • Yandex Forms — receiving messages through the feedback form. Policy: yandex.ru/legal/forms_termsofuse

Data processing by both services complies with 152-FZ. Storage servers are located in the Russian Federation.

🗄️ What is stored on your device

  • cookie-consent — your choice in the consent banner: accepted or rejected. Without it the banner would appear on every visit.
  • theme — the selected theme (dark / light), so you do not have to switch it manually.

The site sets and uses no cookies of its own for advertising, profiling or selling data.

🔒 Local storage in Hactar

The Project Hactar stack builder stores your selection only in localStorage your browser. This data is not sent to a server: the site has no database, no accounts and no login.

  • hactarSelectedIds — which supplements you ticked. The key exists so the selection is not lost on page reload or when switching between the “Overview” and “Links” tabs.
  • hactarSavedStacks — your saved stacks with the names you gave them via the “Save stack” button.

Both keys live only in your browser, are not tied to your identity and are not transferred to third parties. They are removed when you clear the cache or site data in your browser settings. The “Clear all” button in Hactar resets the selection; saved stacks are deleted one by one in the “My stacks” list. If you do not use the builder, these keys are never created.

📊 What Yandex.Metrica collects (after consent)

After clicking “Accept”, the counter is loaded: 112465667 (Yandex.Metrica). In this mode it collects:

  • IP address, device data, screen resolution, country and city (approximate, by IP);
  • click map — which page elements visitors click;
  • outbound link clicks;
  • accurate bounce rate;
  • automatic goals — Metrica creates goals for important events (e.g. form submission).

Metrica does not receive personal data (name, phone, e-mail) through this site. Data processing is described in Yandex's policies: yandex.ru/legal/confidential · Metrica documents.

🗓️ Retention periods

  • Yandex.Metrica cookies — 1 year (the service's standard period, set by Yandex).
  • Feedback form data — until your message is answered and no longer than 1 year after that, then destroyed within 30 days.

Legal basis: Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27.07.2006 “On Personal Data”.

🇪🇺 GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)

If you are in the EEA, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679) applies to the processing of your personal data. The site:

  • collects the minimum of personal data (only what you entered in the feedback form);
  • does not transfer personal data to third parties other than Yandex (Metrica, Forms) — and only with your consent;
  • does not profile you or make automated decisions;
  • does not sell or rent user databases.

Your GDPR rights: access, rectification, erasure (“right to be forgotten”), restriction of processing, data portability, objection to processing, complaint to a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, contact us at deadsno1613@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 30 days.

🚫 How to opt out

Cookie analytics

The “Decline” button in the consent banner — Metrica is never loaded: before consent, no request goes to mc.yandex.ru.

The refusal is stored in the key cookie-consent; to bring the choice back, delete it manually:

  • DevTools → Application → Local Storage → this site → the key cookie-consent → Delete;
  • after that the banner will appear again.

You can also block the domain mc.yandex.ru with an extension or in your browser settings — this will not break the site, it runs on open data without any analytics.

Feedback form data

Write to deadsno1613@gmail.com asking to delete the data of your message. Requests are processed within 30 days.

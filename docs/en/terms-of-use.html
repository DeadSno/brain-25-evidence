📌 What this site is

Brain 25 Evidence is a non-commercial educational project. It collects and structures information about studies of dietary supplements: evidence grades, described effects, dosages, interactions and links to publications in PubMed, Europe PMC, CrossRef, OpenAlex and other open sources.

The site does not sell supplements, does not provide medical services and is not a mass media outlet registered in the prescribed manner.

⚠️ Not medical advice

The site materials are purely informational and educational. They are not a diagnosis, a prescription, a treatment plan or a guide to self-treatment.

Dosages and compatibility assessments come from the sources and may be incomplete or inapplicable to your situation: they do not account for your chronic conditions, pregnancy, age, medications and individual reactions.

Make any decision about taking supplements together with a doctor. If you have symptoms or an illness, see a specialist first. Do not delay seeing a doctor because of something you read on this site.

A dietary supplement is not a medicine and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Effects attributed to supplements are studied in research and do not mean a product is right for you.
